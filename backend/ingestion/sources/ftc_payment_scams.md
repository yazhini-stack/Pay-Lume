# FTC Consumer Advice: How to Avoid Scams Involving Peer-to-Peer Payment Apps and Wire Transfers

## Overview
Scammers frequently demand payments via peer-to-peer (P2P) payment services (e.g., Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, PayPal Friends & Family), wire transfers, prepaid debit cards, gift cards, or cryptocurrency. These payment channels are favored because transactions are nearly instantaneous and offer limited to no fraud protection or chargeback mechanisms once authorized.

## Common Payment Scam Methodologies
1. **Fake Overpayment or Accidental Transfer**: The scammer sends a fraudulent notification claiming they accidentally sent money to your account, or sends a fake payment receipt that shows a balance that does not actually exist in your real bank account. They demand an immediate "refund" before the initial deposit bounces.
2. **Imposter Scams (Bank Fraud Departments)**: Fraudsters send an SMS text message appearing to come from your bank's fraud alert system ("Did you attempt a $940 charge? Reply YES or NO"). When you reply, they call claiming to be fraud specialists and guide you to transfer funds to a "safe secondary account" or provide an OTP verification code. Legitimate banks never ask customers to transfer money to protect it.
3. **Advance Fee and Marketplace Scams**: Sellers on online marketplaces insisting solely on non-reversible payment methods before shipping items. Once paid, the seller vanishes and blocks communications.
4. **Demands for Gift Cards or Crypto**: Government agencies, tech support companies, utility companies, and law enforcement never accept payment or fines via gift cards or crypto ATMs. Any demand for gift card PINs is a guaranteed scam.

## Essential Precautions Before Making Any Payment
- Treat P2P apps like digital cash: only send funds to individuals or businesses you know and personally verify in real life.
- Double-check phone numbers, usernames, and recipient identifiers letter-by-letter prior to confirming transfers.
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) and biometric authorization on all banking and financial applications.
